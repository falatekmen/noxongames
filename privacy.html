Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This policy explains what information NoxonGames ("we") collects when you take part in the live streams of the NoxonGames YouTube channel, how we use it, and how you can have it deleted. NoxonGames is run by an individual developer, Falat Hüseyin Ekmen. Contact: livegames8796@gmail.com.

YouTube API Services

NoxonGames uses YouTube API Services to read the live chat of our own live streams. By taking part in our live streams you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's collection and use of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What we collect

Only when you write in the live chat of a NoxonGames live stream, our game server receives from the YouTube API the same public information that every viewer of the stream can see:

  • your YouTube display name and channel ID,
  • the address (URL) of your public profile picture,
  • the text of your chat message and when it was sent, and whether it was a Super Chat.

We do not ask you to sign in, we do not use Google sign-in (OAuth), and we cannot access your YouTube or Google account, your email address, or any private information. Our website uses no cookies, analytics or trackers.

How we use it

  • To turn chat messages into game moves (for example, counting a comment with a country's name as a vote for that country).
  • To show your display name and profile picture in the stream, for example in the list of viewers who voted for a winner or on a record board.
  • To keep the game's state and scores, so that a game can continue after a restart.

We do not sell, rent or share this information with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

  • Game state (channel IDs and display names of recent players, their votes or game words, scores) is kept on our game server while the game it belongs to is running, and is replaced when a new game or championship starts.
  • All-time game records (for example, the greatest tower height, or which secret words have been found) do not contain any viewer's name or ID.
  • Server event logs (game events such as a battle result, which can include a display name) are deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • In line with the YouTube API Services Developer Policies, no information received from the YouTube API is kept for longer than 30 days.
  • The live stream itself, including the chat and what is shown on screen, is published on YouTube and is governed by YouTube's own policies.

Deleting your information

To have any information about you deleted from our game server, email livegames8796@gmail.com with your YouTube display name or channel. We delete it within 7 days and confirm by email. Because we never get access to your account, there is nothing to revoke; if you ever authorize an app through Google, you can review and revoke that access at any time on the Google security settings page.

Children

Our streams are not directed at children under 13, and chat is not available on streams that are made for kids.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
