Live games played by the YouTube chat

NoxonGames is a YouTube channel that streams interactive simulation games. Viewers take part by writing in the live chat of our streams. For example, in Country Battle Royale viewers comment a country's name to put its flag into the next battle, and the names of the viewers who voted for the winner are shown on screen.

Watch NoxonGames on YouTube

How it works

Our game server reads the public live chat of our own NoxonGames live streams through the YouTube API Services (YouTube Data API, live chat messages). It turns chat messages into game moves, and the game is shown in the stream. Viewers do not sign in to anything and we do not access any viewer's YouTube account.

YouTube API Services

NoxonGames uses YouTube API Services. By taking part in our live streams you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. How we handle data is described in our Privacy Policy.
